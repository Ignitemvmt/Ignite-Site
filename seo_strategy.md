# Ignite Belize SEO Strategy & Implementation Guide

## ✅ What We Just Implemented

### 1. **Meta Tags & Open Graph (All Pages)**
- **Enhanced titles** with keywords: "Ignite Belize — Gen-Z Revival, Worship & Youth Ministry"
- **Optimized descriptions** including target keywords (Gen-Z, youth, revival, worship, ministry)
- **Open Graph tags** for better social media sharing (Facebook, LinkedIn)
- **Twitter Card tags** for better Twitter visibility
- **Canonical URLs** to avoid duplicate content issues
- **robots meta tag** to guide search engine behavior

### 2. **Schema Markup (JSON-LD) on Homepage**
- Organization schema with:
  - Name, URL, logo, description
  - Contact email
  - Location (Spanish Lookout, Cayo, Belize)
  - Founder info
  - Tax ID (Belize registration)
- Helps Google understand you're a legitimate nonprofit in Belize

### 3. **sitemap.xml**
- Lists all 4 pages with priority levels
- Tells Google your site structure
- Updated change frequency for each page

### 4. **robots.txt**
- Allows search engines to crawl your site
- Points to your sitemap
- Blocks irrelevant directories (/delete, /new, /.git)

## 🚀 Next Steps (Do These Immediately)

### Step 1: Submit to Google Search Console
1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "URL prefix" and enter: `https://ignitebelize.org/`
3. Verify ownership via HTML file (GitHub Pages compatible)
4. Once verified, submit your sitemap: https://ignitebelize.org/sitemap.xml
5. Request indexing of your homepage

**Why this matters:** Google needs permission to crawl & rank you. This is the fastest way to get indexed.

### Step 2: Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools
1. Go to https://www.bing.com/webmasters/
2. Add your site
3. Submit your sitemap
4. Verify DNS or meta tag

**Why:** Bing powers Yahoo, AOL, and ~15% of searches. Easy win.

### Step 3: Monitor Ranking Progress
Use these free tools:
- **Google Search Console** - See search queries, impressions, clicks
- **Ubersuggest** (free tier) - Track keyword rankings weekly
- **MozBar** browser extension - See domain authority metrics
